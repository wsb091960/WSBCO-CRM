How the ranking works

{{metric}} = age-64 population estimate ÷ ({{'all Medicare CRM contacts' if f.lens == 'presence' else 'distinct contacts with recent recorded outreach'}} + 1). Adding 1 keeps counties with no contacts comparable without dividing by zero. Indices use the same fixed map scale across all four states.

Recent outreach includes saved call attempts, activities explicitly logged as calls or emails, and the CRM last-contact field. A contact is counted once even when several events are recorded. Notes, status changes, scheduled follow-ups, failed call setup and future dates do not count. Saved call totals come only from call logs; a call/email activity can establish outreach without adding a call log.

CRM contact counts include all ages, including missing birth dates and Do Not Call records, and use the existing Medicare workspace membership rule. These are market-planning indices, not sales probabilities, market-share percentages or counts of uncontacted people. Missing history can make a county appear underworked.

Address data quality

{{coverage.unresolved}} Medicare contacts in {{state_name}} have an unresolved county, a changed address awaiting verification or a county conflict. They are excluded from county counts. Review address data.
