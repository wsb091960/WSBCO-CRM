How It Works

Agent-first calling prevents the prospect from hearing dead air.

  1. Click Call Next Lead.
  2. Twilio calls your agent phone.
  3. Answer and hear the connection message.
  4. Twilio dials the contact.
  5. Save the disposition and follow-up.
